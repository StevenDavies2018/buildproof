"Should I launch on Kickstarter?" isn't really one question — it's shorthand for a handful of narrower, more answerable ones: how often do campaigns like mine succeed, does my category matter, and what am I about to get right or wrong on goal and timing? We pulled the real numbers behind each of those from Backer Sonar's full dataset instead of answering in the abstract.

## The baseline

Across every completed Kickstarter campaign in the dataset (successful or unsuccessful, all categories, campaigns launched between April 2009 and August 2026):

**107,620 of 178,871 completed campaigns succeeded — a 60.2% overall success rate.**

That's the honest floor to start from before anything else. It's higher than the headline "roughly 1 in 3" figure you'll see quoted elsewhere, largely because most public figures count *all* campaigns Kickstarter has ever hosted (including a large volume of very early, low-effort, or abandoned projects) rather than a cleaned, currency-normalized, deduplicated dataset like this one.

## Does category matter?

Yes, substantially. Here's the same all-category dataset broken out by parent category:

| Category | Campaigns | Success rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Art | 7,443 | 80.3% |
| Comics | 7,084 | 80.3% |
| Music | 16,351 | 74.4% |
| Games | 15,748 | 74.1% |
| Design | 8,974 | 66.5% |
| Film & Video | 27,797 | 63.6% |
| Publishing | 21,133 | 62.9% |
| Theater | 6,360 | 57.5% |
| Dance | 2,004 | 60.6% |
| Technology | 16,855 | 51.4% |
| Photography | 6,949 | 49.6% |
| Fashion | 10,952 | 48.5% |
| Food | 15,523 | 37.0% |
| Crafts | 7,200 | 31.4% |
| Journalism | 4,390 | 23.5% |

The spread is wide — Journalism campaigns succeed at roughly a quarter of the rate of Art or Comics campaigns. Some of this reflects genuinely different backer psychology (a $10 enamel pin reward is a much easier "yes" than funding an ongoing news outlet), and some of it reflects typical goal sizes varying by category — which is exactly what the next section gets at.

## What moves the needle more than category

Two factors we've broken down in separate posts turn out to matter as much or more than category alone:

- **Goal size.** Across the same dataset, campaigns asking for under $1,000 succeed 78.0% of the time; campaigns asking for $150,000+ succeed only 9.6% of the time. See [Does a Lower Kickstarter Goal Increase Your Odds of Success?](/blog/does-a-lower-kickstarter-goal-increase-your-odds) for the full breakdown.
- **Campaign duration.** Short campaigns (15 days or fewer) succeed at 75.6%, versus 51.5% for long campaigns (30+ days). See [Kickstarter Campaign Length: Short vs. Long](/blog/kickstarter-campaign-length-short-vs-long) for the details.

If you're in a lower-performing category like Food or Crafts, a conservative goal and a shorter window are exactly the levers the data suggests are worth leaning on — they move the odds more than category alone dictates.

## What this doesn't tell you

- **Category isn't randomly assigned either.** Creators self-select into categories based on what they're already building, so a low success rate in Journalism or Crafts partly reflects the inherent difficulty of funding those project types via a small-reward crowdfunding model — not that the category is a mistake to attempt.
- **This is a point-in-time snapshot of a live dataset.** Success rates shift as Kickstarter's audience and typical campaign quality change over time; the split here reflects everything resolved as of this dataset's last update, not a fixed law.
- **None of this replaces category-specific research.** If you're building a TTRPG project specifically, see the [TTRPG subcategory breakdown](/blog/ttrpg-kickstarter-subcategories-by-success-rate) for a narrower, more relevant slice of this same data.

## What this is useful for

"Should I launch" is really "should I launch *this*, structured *this* way." The data says category, goal size, and duration all move your odds — often by more than doubling or halving them. If you want to check where your specific idea falls before committing, you can filter this same dataset by category, goal, and duration in [Backer Sonar's reports](/reports/structure).
