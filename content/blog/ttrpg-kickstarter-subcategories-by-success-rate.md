TTRPG campaigns succeed on Kickstarter at a strikingly high rate overall — 90.3% across the 2,229 classified TTRPG campaigns in Backer Sonar's dataset with a median goal of just $500. That's far above the roughly 60% success rate across the full Kickstarter dataset at large. But "TTRPG" isn't one market — it's a set of fairly different subcategories, and they don't all perform the same way.

## The pattern within the pattern

Even inside a category that already succeeds far more often than Kickstarter overall, the same relationship from our [goal-size analysis](/blog/does-a-lower-kickstarter-goal-increase-your-odds) shows up again: the two lowest-performing subcategories, **DM Tools** (70.1%) and **Campaign Settings** (75.9%), also carry the highest median goals ($1,200 and $1,243) — more than double the median across most other subcategories. The highest performers (Maps, Terrain, Miniatures, Visual) cluster at median goals under $200.

This doesn't mean DM Tools or Campaign Settings projects are inherently weaker ideas — these are often more ambitious, higher-production-cost projects (software tooling, full setting books) that legitimately require a bigger ask. But if you're planning a campaign in one of these subcategories, it's worth knowing you're operating in a segment with a meaningfully lower historical success rate than TTRPG as a whole, and sizing expectations accordingly.

## What this is useful for

If you're deciding which corner of the TTRPG space to launch in, or how to position a campaign that spans more than one of these subcategories, this ranking is a starting point for calibrating expectations — not a verdict on any individual project. You can explore the underlying campaigns behind any of these numbers directly in [Backer Sonar's Reporting view](/reports).
