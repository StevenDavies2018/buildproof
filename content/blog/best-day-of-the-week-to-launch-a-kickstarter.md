---
title: The Best Day of the Week to Launch a Kickstarter
description: We pulled launch timestamps for 181,464 completed Kickstarter campaigns and broke down both launch volume and success rate by day of the week. One day stands out on both counts.
publishedAt: "2026-08-24"
---

Launch day gets debated constantly in creator communities, usually as a guess dressed up as advice. We checked it directly: every completed campaign in Backer Sonar's dataset with a recorded launch timestamp, grouped by day of the week, across all categories.

## The numbers

| Day | Campaigns launched | Success rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tuesday | 41,838 | 66.7% |
| Wednesday | 30,080 | 59.7% |
| Monday | 29,608 | 58.9% |
| Thursday | 26,886 | 59.5% |
| Friday | 26,273 | 58.1% |
| Saturday | 14,560 | 55.2% |
| Sunday | 12,219 | 54.0% |

**Tuesday wins on both counts.** It's the single most popular launch day &mdash; nearly 40% more campaigns launch on a Tuesday than on the next-closest day &mdash; and it also has the highest success rate at 66.7%, roughly 7 to 12 points above every other weekday. Weekend launches are both the rarest and the weakest performers, with Sunday landing lowest on both volume and success rate.

## What this doesn't prove

- **Popularity and performance are entangled here.** Tuesday isn't just the best-performing day, it's also the most crowded one. Some of that 66.7% may reflect that more experienced, better-prepared creators have converged on Tuesday as a default, not that the day itself carries some inherent advantage.
- **Weekday vs. weekend isn't a clean causal story either.** Weekend launches are rarer partly because platforms, press, and backers are less active on Saturday/Sunday &mdash; a real distribution effect, not proof that a Saturday launch dooms a specific project.
- **This is an all-category, all-time view.** Launch-day patterns can shift by category or by year; we haven't sliced this further yet.

## What this is useful for

If you have flexibility on launch date and no stronger reason to pick otherwise, the data favors a weekday launch &mdash; Tuesday specifically &mdash; over a weekend one. It's one input among several: see our posts on [goal size](/blog/does-a-lower-kickstarter-goal-increase-your-odds) and [campaign duration](/blog/kickstarter-campaign-length-short-vs-long) for the other levers the data says matter more.

You can explore campaign timing yourself, filtered by category and year, in [Backer Sonar's reports](/reports/structure).
